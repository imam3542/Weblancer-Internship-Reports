Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, with internal convective motion that generates a magnetic field via a dynamo process. It is by far the most important source of energy for life on Earth. Its diameter is about 1.39 million kilometers, or 109 times that of Earth, and its mass is about 330,000 times that of Earth, accounting for about 99.86% of the total mass of the Solar System.

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in the Solar System and the closest to the Sun. It is a rocky planet with a thin atmosphere, and its surface is covered in craters. Mercury has a diameter of about 4,880 kilometers, making it only slightly larger than the Moon. It has no moons of its own and takes about 88 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is Earth's closest neighbor. It is similar in size and composition to Earth, but its atmosphere is extremely thick and toxic, composed mainly of carbon dioxide, with clouds of sulfuric acid. Venus has a surface temperature hot enough to melt lead, making it the hottest planet in the Solar System. It takes about 225 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet to support life. It has a diverse range of ecosystems, including forests, oceans, deserts, and polar regions. Earth has a diameter of about 12,742 kilometers and is the fifth-largest planet in the Solar System. It has one moon, known as the Moon, which orbits the planet every 27.3 days.

Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite and the fifth-largest moon in the Solar System. It is about 3,474 kilometers in diameter and is located an average of 384,400 kilometers from Earth. The Moon has a rocky surface covered in craters, and it has no atmosphere to speak of. It takes about 27.3 days for the Moon to complete one orbit around Earth.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often referred to as the "Red Planet" due to its reddish appearance. It is a rocky planet with a thin atmosphere, and its surface is covered in deserts, canyons, and polar ice caps. Mars has a diameter of about 6,779 kilometers and has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. It takes about 687 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Phobos

Phobos is one of the two moons of Mars, the other being Deimos. It is the larger and closer of the two moons, with a diameter of about 22 kilometers. Phobos is irregularly shaped and heavily cratered, and it orbits Mars at an extremely close distance, taking only about 7 hours and 39 minutes to complete one orbit.

Deimos

Deimos is the smaller and more distant of the two moons of Mars, the other being Phobos. It has a diameter of about 12 kilometers and is also irregularly shaped and heavily cratered. Deimos orbits Mars at a greater distance than Phobos, taking about 30.3 hours to complete one orbit.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and is the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant with a thick atmosphere composed mainly of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter has a diameter of about 139,822 kilometers and is known for its Great Red Spot, a massive storm that has been raging for hundreds of years. Jupiter has at least 79 moons, with the four largest being known as the Galilean moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is known for its stunning rings. It is a gas giant with a diameter of about 116,460 kilometers. Saturn's atmosphere is primarily composed of hydrogen and helium, and it has at least 82 moons, with Titan being the largest. Saturn takes about 29.5 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is classified as an ice giant. It has a diameter of about 50,724 kilometers and is known for its unique blue-green color, which is due to the presence of methane in its atmosphere. Uranus has at least 27 moons and takes about 84 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun in the Solar System. It is also classified as an ice giant and has a diameter of about 49,244 kilometers. Neptune's atmosphere is primarily composed of hydrogen, helium, and methane, giving it a deep blue color. It has at least 14 moons, with Triton being the largest. Neptune takes about 165 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.
